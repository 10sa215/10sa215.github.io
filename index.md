## 🛠️ Habits That Get the Most Out of the Toolkit

- **Update after every session, not every week.** Notes written ten minutes after a run are far more accurate than notes written from memory three days later.
- **Keep two builds alive at once.** One aggressive, one safe. Comparing them teaches you more than perfecting a single build ever will.
- **Name your builds clearly.** "Shotgun rush v2" tells you far more than "Build 4" when you come back a month later.
- **Rate your practice honestly.** A low rating on a hard session is useful data, not a failure.
- **Revisit your arena plans after a loss.** Adding a single retreat marker often fixes a route that keeps getting you cornered.
